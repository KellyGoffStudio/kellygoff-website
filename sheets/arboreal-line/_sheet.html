Project statement

Arboreal Line is a single continuous line of Schedule 40 aluminum pipe, 8 in. and 4 in. in diameter. It rises from a concrete planter on the sidewalk as a straight stem, then loops back on itself high above the street. At 18 feet tall, it reads as a drawing in space, from across the street and from underneath.

The work is finished in gloss white marine epoxy. It was installed in 2023 as a permanent work for Uptown Trees in Greeley, Colorado.
